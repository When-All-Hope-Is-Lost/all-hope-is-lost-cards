## Do this now
**Call Poison Help 1-800-222-1222 (free, 24/7) right away. If the person collapses, has a seizure, has trouble breathing, or can't be woken up, call 911.** You can also use the free webPOISONCONTROL online tool at poison.org. Don't wait for symptoms.

## Steps
1. **Get the person away from the poison.** Remove anything left in the mouth; have them spit it out.
2. **Call 1-800-222-1222** (or 911 for the emergencies above). Have ready:
   - Age and approximate weight
   - What was swallowed (bring the container, label, or a plant/mushroom sample)
   - How much, and when
   - Symptoms now, and any health conditions or medicines
3. **Do exactly what the poison specialist says.**
4. **If the person stops breathing,** start CPR (see the CPR cards).

## If / then
- **If** the product burns or irritates (caustic cleaner, bleach, drain opener) and the person is awake, not seizing, and able to swallow **then** give a small amount of water or milk right away, then call (Poison Control). Otherwise give nothing by mouth unless the poison center tells you to.
- **If** it was a button battery or magnet **then** go to the emergency room now.
- **If** it was a wild mushroom or plant **then** call even if they feel fine; some deadly ones cause no symptoms for hours.
- **If** there's no phone or signal **then** get the person to the nearest emergency room, clinic, or anyone with a working phone or radio as fast as is safe. Bring the poison or its container. Keep them on their side if they're drowsy or vomiting, and watch their breathing.

## Don't
- **Don't make them vomit.** Not with fingers, salt water, mustard or anything else. It can cause choking and burns and doesn't remove enough poison.
- **Don't use syrup of ipecac.** The American Academy of Pediatrics (2003) and toxicology groups no longer recommend it at home.
- **Don't give activated charcoal** unless a poison center or clinician tells you to.
- **Don't try home "flushes"** (large amounts of water or milk beyond that small sip, oil, vinegar, laxatives, or "detox" remedies). There is no safe home antidote.
- **Don't wait for symptoms.**

## Get help now
Poison Help **1-800-222-1222** (US, 24/7, free, interpreters available). Emergency **911**.
