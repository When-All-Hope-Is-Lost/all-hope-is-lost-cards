## Do this now
**Swelling in the jaw, face, or neck with fever, or any trouble breathing, swallowing, or opening your mouth? Go to an emergency room or call 911 now.** A tooth infection can spread and become life-threatening.

## Steps
1. **See a dentist as soon as you can** for a throbbing toothache that won't go away, or a pimple-like bump on the gum. An abscess usually needs a dentist to treat it, with antibiotics, draining it, a root canal, or pulling the tooth.
2. **Until then, for comfort:** rinse with **warm salt water**, and take an over-the-counter pain reliever as the label directs. It can also bring down a fever.
3. **No dentist or money?** Look for a federally funded **health center** (findahealthcenter.hrsa.gov). Many offer dental care with fees based on income. Call **2-1-1** for local options.
4. **Keep the area clean.** Brush gently with clean water (see the sanitation card).

## If / then
- **If** the pain suddenly stops **then** don't assume it's healed. Pressure may have been relieved, but the infection can still be spreading. Still see a dentist.
- **If** you have **swelling of the upper or lower jaw** **then** treat it as **very serious**. Get care today.
- **If** you have fever, swollen neck glands, or feel generally ill with a bad tooth **then** get care promptly.
- **If** a tooth is broken or chipped **then** have it checked soon. Cracks let bacteria in.

## Don't
- **Don't put aspirin (or any pill) directly on the tooth or gum.** It burns the tissue and can cause sores.
- Don't wait out a swollen jaw or face.

## Get help now
**911 or the ER** for facial or neck swelling with fever, or for trouble breathing or swallowing. Dentist or health center for everything else. **2-1-1** to find low-cost dental care.
