## Do this now
Not responding and not breathing normally? **Shout for help and start pushing on the chest** (2 fingers for a baby; 1 or 2 hands for a child) **hard and fast**.

## Steps
1. **Check:** tap and shout. Not responding and not breathing normally (or only gasping)? Start CPR.
2. **Call for help:** have someone **call 911** and find an AED. **If you're alone and didn't see the child collapse**, do **about 2 minutes of CPR first** (about 5 rounds), then call 911.
3. **Position:** lay them face up on a firm, flat surface.
4. **Hands:**
   - **Baby (under 1):** 2 fingers in the center of the chest, just below the nipple line.
   - **Child:** the heel of 1 hand (or 2 hands for a bigger child) in the center of the chest.
5. **Depth:** push down **about one-third of the chest's depth**: about **1½ inches (4 cm) for a baby** and about **2 inches (5 cm) for a child**.
6. **Speed: 100 to 120 a minute.** Let the chest come all the way back up each time.
7. **Breaths:** after **30 pushes**, tilt the head back slightly and lift the chin (for a baby, only slightly), then give **2 gentle breaths**. For a baby, you may need to cover the mouth and nose with your mouth Each breath should just make the chest rise.
8. **Keep going** (30 pushes, 2 breaths) until the child breathes or moves, help takes over, or an AED is ready.

## If / then
- **If** an AED arrives **then** turn it on and follow its voice prompts (see the AED card). Use child pads for a child under 8 if available.
- **If** you won't or can't give breaths **then** keep pushing. Rescue breaths matter more for children than for adults, because their hearts usually stop from a breathing problem.
- **If** the child was drowning or choking **then** breaths are especially important. With choking, check the mouth before breaths and remove only what you can see.
- **If** they start breathing **then** roll them onto their side and watch them until help arrives.

## Don't
- Don't push too gently. Shallow pushes don't move blood.
- Don't tilt a baby's head far back. Babies aren't positioned with the full head tilt used for adults.
- Don't stop to recheck over and over. Keep going.

## Get help now
**911** every time. If you're alone with a child who's already unresponsive, give 2 minutes of CPR, then call.
