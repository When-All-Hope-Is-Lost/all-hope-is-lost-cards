## Do this now
If they can't breathe, cough, or speak, call for help and start **5 back blows, then abdominal thrusts**.

## Steps
1. **Ask "Are you choking?"** If they can cough hard or talk, let them keep coughing and stay with them.
2. If they **can't breathe, can't speak, or are making high-pitched sounds**, act now. Have someone **call 911**.
3. **Give 5 back blows** first.
4. **Abdominal thrusts:** stand behind them and wrap your arms around their waist. Make a fist and put the thumb side **just above the belly button, well below the breastbone**. Grab your fist with your other hand.
5. **Thrust quickly inward and upward.** Each thrust is a separate, distinct push.
6. **Keep going** until the object comes out or they pass out.

## If / then
- **If** the person is heavily pregnant, very large (you can't reach around), or has a belly wound **then** use **chest thrusts**: arms under their armpits, fist in the middle of the breastbone (not the tip or the ribs), and thrust inward.
- **If** they become unresponsive **then** call 911 (if no one has), lower them to the ground, and start CPR (see the CPR card).
- **If** the object comes out **then** they should still be checked by a medical professional, because thrusts can cause injury.

## Don't
- Don't step in while they can still cough forcefully and breathe. Act when air exchange is poor or stops.
- Don't put your fist on the tip of the breastbone or on the ribs.
- Don't give up. Keep alternating until help arrives or they pass out.

## Get help now
**911** if they can't breathe, turn blue, or become unresponsive.
