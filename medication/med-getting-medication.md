## Do this now
**Call your pharmacist** and say you can't afford or can't get your medication. Ask what your options are today.

## Steps
1. **Ask the pharmacist:** "Is there a cheaper generic or a different size? What's the cash price? Can you fill part of it now?"
2. **Find a community health center** at findahealthcenter.hrsa.gov (or ask 2-1-1). HRSA-funded health centers **serve you whether or not you can pay** and charge on a **sliding fee** based on income and family size. At or below the federal poverty line, care is free or a small nominal fee. Ask whether they have a pharmacy or discount drug program.
3. **Lost your job's insurance?** You have **60 days** from losing job-based coverage to pick a HealthCare.gov plan (Special Enrollment). Or you can elect **COBRA** within **60 days**, usually paying the full premium.
4. **Apply for Medicaid** (in Missouri, MO HealthNet) through your state or HealthCare.gov. Eligibility depends on your **current monthly income**, so you may qualify the month your income drops.
5. **Call your prescriber's office** and tell them about cost. They may switch you to a cheaper medicine or give samples (ask).

## If / then
- **If** you're about to run out of a life-sustaining medicine (insulin, heart, seizure, transplant, or psychiatric medication) **then** tell the pharmacist and prescriber that it's urgent, today.
- **If** you have no doctor **then** a health center can see you and write prescriptions.
- **If** you're feeling hopeless about your health or money **then** call or text **988**.

## Don't
- Don't stop or change your medicines until you talk to your doctor or pharmacist (CDC gives this advice for hot weather, and it applies here too).
- Don't take someone else's prescription medicine.
- Don't buy from websites that sell prescription medicine **without a prescription**, aren't licensed by a US state board of pharmacy, or offer prices that seem too good to be true. FDA warns that these may be unsafe (check at fda.gov/besaferx).

## Get help now
**911** for trouble breathing, chest pain, a seizure, or confusion, including after missing doses. Poison Help **1-800-222-1222** if you took the wrong medicine or too much. **2-1-1** for local help.
