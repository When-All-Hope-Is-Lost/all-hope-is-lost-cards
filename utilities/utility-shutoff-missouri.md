## Do this now
**Call your utility today**, before the shutoff date, and ask for a **payment plan**. Then call **2-1-1** about energy assistance (LIHEAP).

## Steps
1. **Read the notice:** find the shutoff date, the amount due, and the utility's phone number.
2. **Call the utility before that date.** Say: "I can't pay the full amount. I want to set up a payment plan, and I want to know about the Cold Weather Rule and any assistance."
3. **Apply for LIHEAP** (federal home energy help for low-income households). Call **2-1-1**, or start at acf.hhs.gov/ocs/programs/liheap to find your state's office. Ask about **crisis** assistance if shutoff is close.
4. **Ask 211** about local utility-help funds and charities.
5. **Write down** every call: date, the person's name, and what they promised.
6. **Keep up with the plan** once you're on it. Breaking the agreement can restart the shutoff process.

## If / then
- **If** you're in Missouri **between Nov 1 and Mar 31** with a regulated gas or electric utility **then** the **Cold Weather Rule** applies:
  - Heat-related service can't be shut off on any day the **National Weather Service local forecast** (made between 6 a.m. and 9 p.m.) predicts **below 32°F at any point in the next 72 hours**. This window was 24 hours before the rule was amended, effective March 30, 2026.
  - You can ask for a **12-month payment plan**, and past-due amounts can be spread over 12 months.
  - The utility must mail you a notice and then try to contact you again (a second notice, door hanger, or calls) within the **96 hours** before disconnection.
  - It may not shut you off if you contact it, say you can't pay in full, make the required first payment, and enter a payment agreement.
- **If** you're a **registered elderly or disabled** customer with income **below 150% of the federal poverty guideline** **then** ask to register. If you're on a payment plan and pay at least 50% of what's due, you may be protected for the whole cold season.
- **If** your power or gas comes from a **city utility, rural co-op, or delivered propane** **then** the Cold Weather Rule does **not** apply (the PSC doesn't regulate them). Ask that provider about its own winter policy, and still apply for LIHEAP.
- **If** the utility refuses or you think it broke the rules **then** contact the **Missouri Public Service Commission** (psc.mo.gov) consumer services.
- **If** the heat is off **then** see the shelter card (warming centers, hypothermia). **Never** heat with a generator, grill, or camp stove indoors.

## Don't
- Don't ignore the notice or wait until the shutoff day.
- Don't agree to a plan you can't afford. Say what you *can* pay.
- Don't run a generator in your home, basement, or garage, or within 20 feet of windows, doors, or vents (CDC).

## Get help now
**2-1-1** for energy help. **911** if someone shows carbon monoxide signs (headache, dizziness, nausea, confusion) or hypothermia. **988** if you're in crisis.
