## Do this now
**Always put a charge controller and a fuse between any charging source and the battery.**

## Steps
1. Match the charge controller to the battery type (flooded lead-acid, AGM, lithium) and system voltage.
2. Fuse every positive lead near the battery.
3. Charge lead-acid in a ventilated place, away from flames and sparks.
4. Wear eye protection when working on batteries.
5. Connect the last cable away from the battery and disconnect it first.
6. Keep batteries off bare ground in cold weather and protect them from freezing when discharged.
7. Run inverters only for loads within their rating; they drain the bank fast.

## If / then
- **If** acid splashes on skin or eyes **then** flush with lots of water for at least 15 minutes and get medical help
- **If** a lithium battery swells, hisses, or smokes **then** get away, move people out, call 911; do not use water on a large pack unless firefighters say so

## Don't
- Never backfeed a house panel. Use a transfer switch installed by an electrician.
- Don't mix old and new batteries or different types in one bank.
- Don't run gasoline generators indoors or in a garage; carbon monoxide kills.

## Get help now
- Fire, explosion, or chemical burn: call 911.
