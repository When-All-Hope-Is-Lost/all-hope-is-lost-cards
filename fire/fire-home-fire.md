## Do this now
**Get out, stay out, and call 911 from outside.** Crawl low under the smoke. A fire can become deadly in **2 minutes**.

## Steps
1. **Get everyone out fast.** Every second counts, and a home can be engulfed in flames in 5 minutes.
2. **Crawl low** under the smoke. Smoke and poison gases collect near the ceiling first.
3. **Before opening a door,** feel the doorknob and the door. If either is hot, or smoke is coming around the door, **keep it closed** and use your second way out.
4. **Open doors slowly,** ready to slam them shut if there's heavy smoke or fire.
5. **Once you're out, stay out.** Call **911** from outside. Tell them if anyone or any pet is still inside, and where.
6. **Don't go back in.** Wait for the fire department to say it's safe.

## If / then
- **If** you can't get out **then** close the door, and cover the vents and the cracks around the door with cloth or tape. Call 911 and tell them where you are. Signal from the window with a light-colored cloth or a flashlight.
- **If** your clothes catch fire **then** **stop, drop, and roll**: drop to the ground, cover your face with your hands, and roll until the fire is out. If someone can't roll, smother the flames with a blanket or towel. Cool the burn and get help (see the burns card).
- **If** the fire is **small and contained** (a pan or a wastebasket), everyone has been warned, 911 has been called, you're clear of the smoke, **and** you have a clear way out behind you **then** you may use an extinguisher. Remember **PASS**:
  - **Pull** the pin.
  - **Aim** low at the base of the fire.
  - **Squeeze** the lever slowly and evenly.
  - **Sweep** side to side.

  Leave if it doesn't go out fast, or if the extinguisher empties.
- **If** you're unsure, or for anything bigger **then** **just get out.** Young children and frail older adults shouldn't use extinguishers (USFA).

## Don't
- Don't hide (teach children not to hide from firefighters), and don't go back inside for anything.
- **Never disable a smoke alarm** while cooking.
- Don't heat your home with a stove or oven. Keep space heaters 3 feet from anything that can burn, and stay in the kitchen while frying, grilling, or broiling.

## Get help now
**911** from outside. **Prevent it:**
- Put **smoke alarms on every level**, including the basement.
- Replace batteries **twice a year** (unless they're 10-year lithium) and **replace the alarms every 10 years**.
- Plan **two ways out of every room**, and practice your escape plan **twice a year**.
- **Sleep with the bedroom door closed.**
