## Do this now
**Crash:** stop, turn on hazard lights, and call **911** if anyone is hurt. **Stranded in snow:** **stay with your car.** **Water over the road:** **turn around, don't drown.**

## Steps
**After a crash**
1. **Stop.** If no one is hurt and the car can move, pull out of the travel lanes onto the shoulder. Turn off the engine.
2. **Warn traffic** with hazard lights, flares, triangles, or a flashlight.
3. **Call 911** right away if anyone is injured or killed. Report the crash to police.
4. **Help the injured,** but **don't pull someone out of a wrecked car** unless there's immediate danger such as fire. Control heavy bleeding (see the severe bleeding card).

**Stranded in snow or ice**
5. **Stay in the car.** It's shelter, and it's easier for rescuers to find you than someone on foot.
6. **Make yourself visible:** tie a bright cloth to the antenna or door, keep the dome light on at night, and raise the hood once the snow stops falling.
7. **Run the engine about 10 minutes each hour** for heat. **Clear snow from the tailpipe** first and crack a window slightly, so carbon monoxide doesn't build up.
8. **Keep moving:** flex your arms and legs to keep blood flowing, but **don't overexert.** Huddle together and use blankets, newspapers, or floor mats for insulation.

## If / then
- **If** the car is skidding on ice **then** ease off the gas and steer gently in the direction you want the front of the car to go. With anti-lock brakes, press the brake steadily and don't pump it.
- **If** you come to water over the road **then** **turn around.** Six inches of moving water can knock you down, and **one foot of moving water can sweep a car away.**
- **If** your car is trapped in rapidly moving water **then** stay inside. If water is rising inside the car, get out and onto the roof.
- **If** a passenger shows signs of hypothermia (shivering, confusion, slurred speech) **then** warm them slowly and get help (see the cold injury card).

## Don't
- **Don't walk away from a stranded car** in a storm unless help is visible and very close.
- **Don't run the engine with a blocked tailpipe.**
- Don't drive around flood barricades, or onto flooded bridges.

## Get help now
**911** (in some places you can text 911 if you can't call). **Keep a car kit:**
- jumper cables
- flashlight and batteries
- blankets
- water and snacks
- a first aid kit
- an ice scraper and a small shovel
- a phone charger
- sand or cat litter for traction
