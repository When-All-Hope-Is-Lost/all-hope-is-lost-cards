## Do this now
Stay alert and watch your surroundings. If you see something unusual, tell staff, security, or local authorities. Call 911 if anyone is in danger.

## Steps
1. **Be aware of your environment.** Know where exits are and stay ready to move quickly if needed.
2. **Use the "Power of Hello" (CISA's OHNO steps):** Observe what's going on, Initiate a calm hello if it's safe, Navigate the risk, and Obtain help.
3. **Walk away if things get tense.** If you feel unsafe, leave the area and find a safe place.
4. **Report suspicious behavior:** unattended packages, threatening behavior, or threatening messages in person or online. Tell local authorities ("If you see something, say something").
5. **If you can't leave, hide.** Get out of view, put a solid barrier between you and the threat, lock and block doors, turn off lights, and stay silent.
6. **Only fight as a last resort.** If you can't run or hide, try to stop the threat. Commit to it; Ready.gov lists everyday objects like chairs or fire extinguishers.

## If / then
- **If** you see someone acting strangely or threatening others **then** keep your distance and tell staff, security, or police.
- **If** you are in a crowd and feel unsafe **then** move toward an exit and stay calm.
- **If** you are in a place where people are fighting **then** leave the area and call 911 when you are safe.
- **If** you are in a situation where someone is attacking **then** run if you can. If not, hide or fight only if you have no other choice.

## Don't
- Don't ignore suspicious behavior.
- Don't go back for belongings. Leave them behind and get away.
- Don't stay in a place that feels unsafe.

## Get help now
- **911**: call if you are in immediate danger.
- **See Something Say Something**: report suspicious activity to local authorities.
- **When police arrive:** keep your hands empty and visible and follow their directions.
