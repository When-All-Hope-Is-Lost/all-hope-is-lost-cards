## Do this now
If anyone is in danger right now, call **911**. If not, step away from the person and call or text **988** to talk it through.

## Steps
1. **Create distance now:** go to another room, step outside, or take a walk. Having a thought isn't the same as acting on it.
2. **Move anything that could be used to hurt someone** out of reach or out of the home (give it to a trusted person if you can).
3. **Call or text 988.** It's free, confidential, and 24/7, and you don't have to be suicidal to call. 988 counselors provide emotional support, crisis de-escalation, and local resources (SAMHSA).
4. **If it's about a partner or home:** National Domestic Violence Hotline, **1-800-799-7233**, or text **START** to **88788**. It also supports people worried they're causing harm.
5. **If a child's safety is at risk:** Childhelp National Child Abuse Hotline, call **1-800-422-4453**, or text **"GO"** to 800-422-4453 (24/7).
6. **Lower the pressure today:** get food (food banks, expedited SNAP, 211) and a safe place to sleep if home isn't safe. Exhaustion and hunger make everything harder.
7. Come back to your plan when you're calmer. You're carrying a lot, and help with that weight is available.

## If / then
- **If** someone is being hurt or you feel you can't stop yourself **then** call **911** now.
- **If** a child is in danger **then** call **911**. For concerns that aren't an emergency, call Childhelp at 1-800-422-4453.
- **If** you're the one being threatened **then** get to safety and call 911, or the DV Hotline (1-800-799-7233 / text START to 88788) when it's safe.
- **If** alcohol or drugs are involved **then** stop using for now, and stay away from others until you're sober.

## Don't
- Don't stay in the room "to win the argument." Leave first, talk later.
- Don't keep weapons or other dangerous items close to hand while you feel this way.
- Don't handle this alone. Calling for help is the responsible move.

## Get help now
- **911**: anyone in immediate danger.
- **988**: call or text, or chat at chat.988lifeline.org.
- **DV Hotline**: 1-800-799-7233 / text START to 88788.
- **Childhelp**: 1-800-422-4453 / text "GO" to 800-422-4453.
