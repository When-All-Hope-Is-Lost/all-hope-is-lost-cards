## Do this now
If you might act on these thoughts soon, call 911 now. Otherwise call or text **988** to talk to someone right away: free, confidential, 24/7.

## Steps
1. **Reach a person now:** call or text **988** (988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline), or chat at **chat.988lifeline.org**. You don't have to be suicidal to call. Money worries count.
2. **Veterans, service members, and families:** call **988 and press 1** (Veterans Crisis Line), or text **838255**.
3. **Prefer texting?** Text **HOME** to **741741** (Crisis Text Line).
4. **Spanish:** call 988 and press 2, or text **AYUDA** to 988. **TTY:** dial 711, then 988.
5. **Put distance between you and anything you could use to hurt yourself:** give it to someone, lock it away, or leave the room.
6. **Don't stay alone if you can help it.** Call or go to someone you trust and tell them, "I'm not safe right now."
7. When you're ready, come back to your plan. This is a hard situation, and it's temporary. There is a real path through it, one step at a time.

## If / then
- **If** you have a plan, or have already hurt yourself **then** call **911** now.
- **If** it isn't safe to call where you are (someone may overhear) **then** text 988 or 741741 instead.
- **If** someone at home is hurting you or scaring you **then** National Domestic Violence Hotline: **1-800-799-7233**, or text **START** to **88788**.
- **If** money, food, or housing is the weight crushing you **then** after reaching out, open the food and shelter cards. Help exists today (211, food banks, expedited SNAP).

## Don't
- Don't wait for it to "get bad enough." You can call 988 for any emotional distress.
- Don't drink or use drugs to get through tonight. Stay clear-headed and reach out instead.
- Don't keep it secret. Telling one person is a strong move, not a weak one.

## Get help now
- **911**: immediate danger, an overdose, or if you've hurt yourself.
- **988**: call or text, or chat at chat.988lifeline.org. Veterans: press 1, or text 838255.
- **Crisis Text Line**: text HOME to 741741.
- **DV Hotline**: 1-800-799-7233, or text START to 88788.
