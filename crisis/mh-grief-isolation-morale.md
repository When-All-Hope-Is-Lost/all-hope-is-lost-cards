## Do this now
**Keep a daily routine and talk to at least one person every day.**

## Steps
1. Grief after loss is normal and comes in waves. Let people talk about who or what they lost.
2. Set a daily routine: wake, work, meals, rest at the same times.
3. Give everyone a useful job, including kids and older adults.
4. Get daylight, move your body, and sleep in regular blocks.
5. Limit alcohol and drugs; they make low mood and conflict worse.
6. Make time for small pleasures: stories, games, music, faith practice.
7. Check on people who go quiet, stop eating, or give things away.

## If / then
- **If** someone talks about wanting to die **then** stay with them, remove means of harm, call or text 988
- **If** you can't sleep or function for weeks **then** reach out to a doctor, counselor, or 988

## Don't
- Don't tell grieving people to 'move on' or how to feel.
- Don't leave someone who is suicidal alone.

## Get help now
- 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline: call or text 988.
- Disaster Distress Helpline: call or text 1-800-985-5990.
- Danger to life: 911.
