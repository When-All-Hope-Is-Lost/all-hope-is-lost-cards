## Do this now
**Keep it clean and cold: chill meat fast and cook poultry and game to 165°F.**

## Steps
1. Learn humane, quick dispatch from an extension class or experienced keeper.
2. Work on clean surfaces with clean knives; wear gloves.
3. Don't puncture the gut; remove organs carefully.
4. Discard animals that look sick, have spotted livers, or acted ill.
5. Chill meat quickly (cold water or ice, then refrigerate or preserve).
6. Cook poultry and rabbit to 165°F (FSIS).

## If / then
- **If** you see white spots on a rabbit's liver **then** discard the carcass; possible tularemia (CDC)
- **If** no refrigeration **then** cook, can, smoke or dry the meat the same day (see preserving cards)

## Don't
- Don't handle wild rabbits barehanded.
- Don't leave meat warm for hours.

## Get help now
- Fever after handling animals: see a doctor and mention the animal contact.
