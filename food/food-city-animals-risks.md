## Do this now
**Go to a food bank or apply for SNAP before eating any wild city animal.** In Missouri, emergency (expedited) SNAP can arrive within 7 days.

## Steps
1. **Know the risk.** CDC says wild rodents spread diseases such as leptospirosis, rat-bite fever, salmonellosis, and hantavirus through handling them or contact with their urine, droppings, or saliva. Their fleas, ticks, and mites spread more.
2. **Don't eat these at all:** rats and mice; pigeons; any animal found dead, acting sick, or unafraid of people; anything from areas with poison bait, trash, sewage, or industrial pollution. Poison and contamination can't be seen and may not cook out.
3. **City squirrels and rabbits:** generally avoid them in towns because of poison bait and pollution. In Missouri they're also regulated game, so seasons and permits apply.
4. **If you handle any wild animal anyway:** wear gloves, don't touch your face, wash your hands with soap, and disinfect surfaces.
5. **If you eat legally taken game, cook it to a safe temperature** checked with a thermometer:
   - **Rabbit and venison: 160°F (71°C)**
   - **Poultry, including game birds: 165°F (74°C)**
   - Ground meat: 160°F
   - Whole cuts of beef, pork, or lamb: 145°F, then rest 3 minutes

## If / then
- **If** a rodent bites or scratches you **then** wash the wound and see a clinician promptly. CDC says rat-bite fever must be treated quickly.
- **If** you get a fever, muscle aches, or breathing trouble after rodent contact **then** get medical care and mention the exposure. Hantavirus symptoms can start 1 to 8 weeks later.
- **If** any wild mammal bit you **then** also see the rabid-animals card.

## Don't
- Don't sweep or vacuum dry rodent droppings. It stirs up contaminated dust; wet them with disinfectant first (CDC cleanup guidance).
- Don't eat any animal found dead.
- Don't trap or shoot animals in city limits without checking the law.

## Get help now
Bite, scratch, fever after rodent contact, or trouble breathing: clinic, urgent care, or **911**. Food today: **2-1-1**.
