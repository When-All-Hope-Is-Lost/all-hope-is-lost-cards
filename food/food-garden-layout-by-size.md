## Do this now
**Start smaller than you think.** Grow a little of the crops your household already eats, and expand next season.

## Steps
1. **How much space per adult** (sourced):
   - University of Idaho Extension says a well-planned **30 x 30 ft (900 sq ft) garden gives fresh vegetables for a family of four**, and you need **about twice that** to can, freeze or preserve.
   - **Estimate** from that: about **225 sq ft per person for fresh eating** and **about 450 sq ft per person to also preserve**.
   - **Full food supply:** a vegetable garden supplies vegetables, not all the calories an adult needs. No figure is given here because none was found in the sources checked. Staples (potatoes, beans, corn, winter squash) give the most food for the space.
2. **How much of each crop per person** (MU Extension, feet of row; fresh use, then fresh plus processing):

| Crop | Fresh use | With canning/storage |
|---|---|---|
| Potato | 50 to 100 ft | (no separate figure) |
| Sweet corn | 15 to 25 ft | 30 to 50 ft |
| Snap beans (bush) | 10 to 15 ft | 20 to 30 ft |
| Carrot | 5 to 10 ft | 10 to 15 ft |
| Onion (dry) | 25 to 50 ft | (no separate figure) |
| Lettuce (leaf) | 5 to 10 ft | (no separate figure) |
| Tomato (staked) | 3 to 5 plants | 5 to 10 plants |
| Pepper | 2 to 3 plants | (no separate figure) |
| Cabbage | 3 to 5 plants | 3 to 5 plants |
| Winter squash | 3 to 5 hills | (no separate figure) |

3. **Layout basics (CSU):** beds 2 to 4 ft wide, any length, with **2-ft walkways**, so you never step on growing soil. Rows suit bigger plots.
4. **Example layouts.** These are suggestions, not from a source; fit them to your own crops.
   - **10 x 10 ft (100 sq ft):** two 4 x 10 ft beds with a 2-ft path. Grow lettuce, carrots, onions, 3 to 5 staked tomatoes, 2 to 3 peppers, and bush beans. That's a fresh-eating supplement for about one person.
   - **20 x 20 ft (400 sq ft):** four or five beds or rows. Add potatoes, a corn block (plant corn in a block of several short rows, not one long row, so it pollinates), and one or two hills of squash at an edge. That's about one adult's fresh vegetables, by the estimate above.
   - **50 x 50 ft (2,500 sq ft):** a row garden. Grow full potato and bean rows, corn, winter squash and cabbage for storage, plus canning tomatoes. By the estimate, it's roughly fresh plus preserving for 4 to 5 people.
5. **Put tall crops (corn, staked tomatoes) on the north side** so they don't shade short crops. *(Common gardening practice; not from the sources above.)*

## If / then
- **If** space is tiny **then** grow leafy greens, herbs, tomatoes and peppers, which give a lot of food per square foot.
- **If** you want stored food for winter **then** plant potatoes, onions, winter squash, beans and canning tomatoes (see food-canning-basics).

## Don't
- Don't plant more than you can weed and water. Abandoned rows waste seed and money.
- Don't count on a vegetable garden as your only food source.
