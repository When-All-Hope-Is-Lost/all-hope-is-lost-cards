## Do this now
**Start small with animals you can feed, water and shelter every day.**

## Steps
1. Chickens: dry predator-proof coop, roost, nest box, daily fresh water and feed, grit; collect eggs daily.
2. Rabbits: shaded, ventilated hutch; hay plus pellets or greens; constant water. They breed quickly.
3. Goats: need a strong fence, shelter, hay, minerals and company of another goat; dairy goats must be milked daily.
4. Wash hands after handling animals or eggs; keep poultry out of the kitchen.
5. Separate sick animals and new animals for a few weeks.
6. Check local rules on keeping animals.

## If / then
- **If** many birds die suddenly **then** isolate the flock and report to the state veterinarian or USDA (possible bird flu)
- **If** an animal stops eating or drinking **then** separate it and seek a vet or extension advice

## Don't
- Don't kiss or snuggle poultry (CDC, Salmonella).
- Don't feed moldy feed or hay.

## Get help now
- Sick or dead birds in numbers: your state veterinarian or USDA APHIS.
