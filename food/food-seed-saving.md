## Do this now
**Save seeds from open-pollinated (heirloom) plants, not hybrids, and store them cool and dry.**

## Steps
1. Easiest to save: beans, peas, lettuce, tomatoes. They mostly self-pollinate.
2. Harder: squash, corn, cabbage family; they cross with neighbors.
3. Save from your best, healthiest plants.
4. Let beans and peas dry on the plant. Scoop tomato seeds, ferment a few days in water, rinse and dry.
5. Dry seeds fully, then store in labeled paper envelopes inside a sealed jar in a cool, dark, dry place.
6. Label crop and year. Test old seeds by sprouting a few on a damp paper towel.

## If / then
- **If** seed packets say F1 or hybrid **then** their seed won't grow true; buy or trade open-pollinated seed
- **If** seeds get moldy **then** they weren't dry enough; discard

## Don't
- Don't store seeds in a hot, humid place.
- Don't eat treated (colored) seeds.

## Get help now
- Local extension office for seed help.
