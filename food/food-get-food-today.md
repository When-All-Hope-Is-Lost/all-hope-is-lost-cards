## Do this now
Find a food pantry near you today with the **Feeding America** locator or by calling **2-1-1**, then apply for SNAP the same day.

## Steps
1. **Food today:** go to feedingamerica.org/find-your-local-foodbank and enter your ZIP. Your food bank lists the pantries and meal sites near you. No internet? **Dial 2-1-1** and ask for food pantries and free meals open today.
2. **HUD Find Shelter** (hud.gov/findshelter) also maps food pantries and clinics near you.
3. **Apply for SNAP (food stamps) now.** In Missouri, apply at **mydss.mo.gov/apply**. Other states: fns.usda.gov/snap/state-directory.
4. **Missouri emergency SNAP ("expedited service"):** answer **Section 2** of the application. In some emergency cases, benefits come in **7 days or less**. You may qualify if:
   - your household's monthly gross income is **under $150 AND** your cash or bank money is **$100 or less**, **or**
   - your income plus cash is **less than your monthly rent or mortgage plus utilities**.
5. **Do the interview fast.** For expedited SNAP, Missouri must finish the interview within **6 days** of your application, or it goes to the normal 30-day timeline. Answer your phone and return calls.
6. **Write down** each place you contact and what they said.

## If / then
- **If** you have kids **then** ask 211 and your school about free meals and WIC.
- **If** you're also without shelter **then** see the shelter card; shelters often serve meals.
- **If** you have no ID or address **then** go to the pantry or 211 anyway, and ask what they accept. Many help regardless.
- **If** hunger is making you feel hopeless or desperate **then** call or text **988**.

## Don't
- Don't wait until you've run out completely. Apply for SNAP the day money gets tight.
- Don't skip the SNAP interview call.
- Don't eat wild-caught city animals, roadkill, or unknown plants or mushrooms to get by. Use food banks first.

## Get help now
**2-1-1** (food, shelter, utilities). **988** if you're in crisis. **911** for emergencies.
