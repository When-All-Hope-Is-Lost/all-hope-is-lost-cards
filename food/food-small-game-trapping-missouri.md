## Do this now
**Before you set anything, check MDC's current trapping regulations and get the right permit.** Illegal sets can mean fines, and they kill pets and protected animals.

## Steps
1. **Read the rules:** MDC Trapping Regulations (mdc.mo.gov) and the current *Summary of Missouri Hunting and Trapping Regulations*.
2. **Get the permit.** Per MDC, the Resident Trapping Permit lets you trap and sell furbearers and groundhogs, and you may also trap rabbits, but **not sell rabbit carcasses**. Some people are exempt (for example, resident landowners on their own land, and residents 15 or younger); check the summary.
3. **Trap only in season.** Furbearer traps may be set from 12:01 a.m. Nov. 15 and must be removed by midnight on the last day of the season. Dates vary by species.
4. **Use only legal devices**, such as smooth- or rubber-jaw foothold traps, cage-type traps, and others MDC lists. A **cage trap** lets you release pets and non-target animals unharmed.
5. **Check traps daily** (killing-type and colony traps at least every 48 hours, per MDC).
6. **Conservation areas** need a Special Use Permit, applied for at least 30 days ahead.

## If / then
- **If** you're thinking of a snare **then** know that in Missouri, snares must be set **underwater** and meet strict specs. **Dry-land snares, deadfalls, pitfalls, and nets are prohibited.**
- **If** you want to use cable restraints **then** they're separately regulated (loop size, height, breakaway, relaxing lock, daily checks, and distance from homes and driveways). Read MDC's cable-restraint rules first.
- **If** you catch a pet or a non-target animal **then** release it unharmed.

## Don't
- Don't trap in town or near homes without checking city rules.
- Don't use improvised snares, poisons, or deadfalls. They're illegal and inhumane.
- Don't eat animals that were sick, acted strangely, or were found dead.

## Get help now
Not sure a method is legal? Call your **MDC regional office**. Hungry today: **2-1-1** or the food-today card.
