## Do this now
**Eat perishables first, keep the fridge and freezer shut, and keep cold food at 40°F or below.**

## Steps
1. **Order of eating:** perishables from the fridge first, then freezer foods, then shelf-stable foods (see utility-power-outage for fridge and freezer hold times).
2. **Use cold you have:** snow keeps food near 32°F, and food sealed in waterproof wrap and tied in a stream stays cool (ATP 3-50.21).
3. **Shady ground is cooler than the surface.** A lined, covered hole works like a small root cellar for short-term storage (ATP 3-50.21).
4. **Evaporative cooling:** wrap food in wet cloth and keep it damp; evaporation cools it a little (ATP 3-50.21).
5. **Protect from animals and insects:** wrap tightly, then hang food several feet off the ground or keep it in sealed containers (ATP 3-50.21).
6. **Dry or preserve surplus meat** the safe way (food-smoking-curing-meat).
7. **Keep canned and dry goods** cool, dry and off the floor.

## If / then
- **If** perishable food has been above 40°F for more than 2 hours **then** throw it out.
- **If** a can is bulging, leaking or spurts when opened **then** throw it out without tasting.

## Don't
- Don't taste food to check whether it's safe.
- Don't store food where floodwater reached it.

## Get help now
Food banks and meal help: **211**. Botulism signs after eating stored food (blurred vision, drooping eyelids, trouble swallowing or breathing): **911**.
