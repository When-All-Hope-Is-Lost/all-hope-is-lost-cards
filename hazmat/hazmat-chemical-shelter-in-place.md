## Do this now
**Follow official instructions right away.** If you're outside, **move upwind** and away from the source. If you're told to stay inside, **close everything and seal one interior room.**

## Steps
1. **Listen to local radio or TV, or your phone's emergency alerts.** Some toxic chemicals have no smell. Officials will tell you whether to **shelter in place** or **evacuate**.
2. **Told to evacuate?** Go **immediately**. To find a public shelter, text **SHELTER** and your ZIP code to **43362**.
3. **Told to stay inside:**
   - Bring pets in.
   - Close and lock exterior doors and windows. Close vents, fireplace dampers, and inside doors.
   - **Turn off air conditioning, heating fans, and ventilation**, or set the system to 100% recirculation.
   - Go to an **interior room** and seal the gaps around doors, windows, vents, and exhaust fans with plastic sheeting, duct tape, or wet towels.
   - If gas may have gotten in, take shallow breaths through a cloth.
4. **Outside?** Find clean air fast: move **upwind** of the source, then get into the nearest building.
5. **In a car?** Keep the windows and vents closed, and turn off the A/C and heater.
6. **Stay inside until officials say it's safe**, even to help others.

## If / then
- **If** you were exposed and medical help isn't available yet **then** decontaminate:
  - Remove all clothing. Cut off anything that would be pulled over your head.
  - Seal the clothing in a plastic bag.
  - Take out contact lenses.
  - Flush your eyes with water.
  - Wash your hands, face, and hair with soap and water, and rinse well.

  Then get medical screening.
- **If** you notice trouble breathing, eye irritation, loss of coordination, nausea, or burning in the nose, throat, or chest, or see many dead birds or insects **then** suspect a chemical release. Get to clean air and call 911.
- **If** the chemical is inside your building **then** get out without passing through the contaminated area if you can. If you can't, move as far from it as possible and shelter there.

## Don't
- Don't go toward the spill, vapor cloud, or crash site to look or help.
- Don't leave a shelter until authorities give the all-clear.

## Get help now
**911** for symptoms or to report a spill. Spills of oil or hazardous materials can also be reported to the **National Response Center: 1-800-424-8802**.
