## Do this now
**When in doubt, follow official guidance, not what you saw in a movie or survival show.**

## Steps
1. Generators: never run one indoors, in a garage, or near windows, even with doors open. CO kills fast and you can't smell it (CDC).
2. Snakebite: keep still and calm, remove rings, keep the limb at about heart level, get to a hospital. No cutting, sucking, tourniquets or ice (CDC).
3. Nuclear fallout: get inside, stay inside, stay tuned. Go to the middle or basement of a solid building for at least 24 hours unless officials say otherwise (Ready.gov).
4. Frostbite: don't rub it or use a heating pad or fire; warm in warm (not hot) water. Alcohol makes you lose heat faster (CDC).
5. Tornado: highway overpasses are not shelter; winds speed up under them (NWS). Get low in a ditch if no building.
6. Fish-tank antibiotics are not tested for people; wrong drug or dose can harm you and breed resistance (FDA).
7. Don't drink urine or seawater; both dehydrate you more.

## If / then
- **If** someone is dizzy, headachy or sleepy near a generator or heater **then** get everyone into fresh air and call 911
- **If** a snakebite victim **then** call 911 or Poison Help 1-800-222-1222 and get to a hospital

## Don't
- Don't trust TV survival tricks for medical emergencies.

## Get help now
- 911.
- Poison Help: 1-800-222-1222.
