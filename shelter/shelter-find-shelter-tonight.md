## Do this now
Call **2-1-1** (or go to hud.gov/findshelter) and ask for an emergency shelter bed tonight. If it's freezing, say so.

## Steps
1. **Call 2-1-1** and ask for emergency shelter, warming centers, and meals. Tell them if you have kids, pets, a disability, or are fleeing abuse.
2. **Or search online** at hud.gov/findshelter (enter a city or ZIP). It lists shelters, food pantries, and clinics.
3. **Fleeing abuse?** Call the **National Domestic Violence Hotline: 1-800-799-7233** or text **START** to **88788**. They can find DV shelters that keep your location private.
4. **Go early.** Beds often fill by evening. Bring ID and any medications if you have them.
5. **If every shelter is full,** ask 211 about warming centers, and stay in staffed public places (hospital lobbies, 24-hour businesses) rather than isolated spots.
6. **Tomorrow:** ask the shelter or 211 about rapid rehousing, rental help, and food (see the food card).

## If / then
- **If** you're sleeping in a car in snow **then** keep the **exhaust pipe clear of snow**, run the engine only about **10 minutes an hour**, and crack a window. Carbon monoxide has no smell or color.
- **If** you use any heater or generator **then** never run it inside a tent, car, garage, or closed space.
- **If** someone is **shivering, exhausted, confused, fumbling, slurring words, or drowsy** (in a baby: bright red, cold skin, very low energy) **then** it may be hypothermia. Get them somewhere warm, remove wet clothes, warm the center of the body first (chest, neck, head, groin), using skin-to-skin contact under dry blankets if needed, and give warm non-alcoholic drinks only if they're awake. Get medical help right away, and call **911** if their temperature is below 95°F.
- **If** they're unconscious and seem to have no pulse or breathing **then** handle them gently, call **911**, and start CPR while you keep warming them. Some people with hypothermia who look dead can be revived (CDC).
- **If** you have a headache, dizziness, nausea, or confusion in a closed space with an engine or heater **then** get to fresh air and call **911** (it may be carbon monoxide).

## Don't
- Don't sleep in a running car in a closed garage, ever.
- Don't drink alcohol to stay warm.
- Don't stay outside in dangerous cold if a warming center exists. Ask 211.

## Get help now
**911** for confusion, unresponsiveness, or suspected carbon monoxide poisoning. **988** if you feel hopeless. **2-1-1** for shelter.
