## Do this now
**Don't ignore court papers. Go to your court date.** Call **legal aid** right away (lsmo.org lists Missouri's legal aid programs). In Missouri, **a landlord can't lock you out, remove your things, or shut off your utilities without a court order.**

## Steps
1. **Read the summons** for your **court date, time, and courtroom**. In a Missouri rent-and-possession case, the court date is usually within **21 business days** of when the summons is issued, and you must be served at least **4 days** before it.
2. **Get legal help now.** Contact your regional legal aid program through **lsmo.org**, or call **2-1-1** for rent-help and housing programs. Ask whether emergency rental assistance is available.
3. **Go to court on time**, even if you think you have no defense. If you don't show up, the judge can rule against you without hearing your side.
4. **Bring your papers:** the lease, rent receipts, bank or money-order records, texts or letters with the landlord, and photos of any repair problems.
5. **If you owe rent and can pay it:** under Missouri law, if you pay **all the rent owed plus court costs** on the day the court enters a money judgment, the case **stops**. (This applies when there's a money judgment, not to every kind of case.)
6. **After a judgment against you,** there are short deadlines. An appeal or new trial won't stop the eviction unless a **bond is posted within 10 days**. Ask legal aid right away.

## If / then
- **If** your landlord **changed the locks, removed your belongings, or shut off your electric, gas, water, or sewer** without a court order **then** that's against Missouri law (RSMo 441.233), apart from narrow health and safety situations. Call legal aid, and call police if you're in danger.
- **If** you get a paper telling you to leave but **no court summons** **then** only a court order can legally remove you. Get legal advice.
- **If** you need somewhere to stay tonight **then** see the shelter card.

## Don't
- **Don't skip your court date.**
- Don't ignore a lockout or utility shutoff by your landlord. Report it and get legal help.
- Don't miss the 10-day window after a judgment if you want to appeal.

## Get help now
**Legal aid:** lsmo.org. **2-1-1** for rent help, utilities, and shelter. **911** if you're threatened or in danger.
This card is general information, **not legal advice**. Laws can change, and your situation may differ.
