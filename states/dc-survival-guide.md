## Do this now
**Water and warmth first, food second. Before you hunt, fish, trap or forage, check the rules: DC Department of Energy and Environment (fisheries and wildlife), https://doee.dc.gov/service/fishing-district**

## Steps
1. **Water.** Treat all surface water (boil 1 minute; see water-emergency-treatment). Known risks here:
- Urban streams carry sewage overflow after storms; never drink untreated.
- DC fish advisory: do not eat eel, carp, striped bass or largemouth bass from DC waters (PCBs, PFAS); limit other species (DOEE).
2. **Heat and shelter.** Climate class: mixed-humid. Both seasons matter: plan for winter heat loss and summer heat illness.
3. **Fish first, then small game.** Fishing usually needs the least gear and energy. Commonly caught here:
- sunfish (limit meals) (seasons: check agency)
- channel and blue catfish (limit meals) (seasons: check agency)
- brown bullhead (limit meals) (seasons: check agency)
4. **Small game** commonly hunted here (license and season required):
- Hunting is not a realistic option here.
- **Note:** DC fish advisory: do NOT eat eel, carp, striped bass or largemouth bass from DC waters (PCBs, PFAS).
5. **Forage only what you can identify with certainty.** Edible plants found here, with native status and lookalike warnings (introduced plants listed last):
- **dandelion** (*Taraxacum officinale*), native species occur here; some types or populations are introduced (USDA PLANTS): Edible, but avoid sprayed lawns, roadsides and industrial sites.
- **mulberry** (*Morus spp.*), native species occur here; some types or populations are introduced (USDA PLANTS): Unripe berries upset the stomach. *(warning not yet checked against an extension source)*
6. **Growing food:** USDA 2023 hardiness zones across this state's ZIP codes: 7b-8a. Look up your ZIP at planthardiness.ars.usda.gov. For local frost dates and planting calendars ask UDC Cooperative Extension Service (https://www.udc.edu/causes/). General schedules, garden size per adult, and canning: see food-garden-planting-calendar, food-garden-layout-by-size and food-canning-basics.
7. **Preserve and store** game safely: see food-smoking-curing-meat and food-storing-without-power.

## If / then
- **If** you are unsure of a plant, mushroom or berry **then** don't eat it; call Poison Help 1-800-222-1222 if someone did.
- **If** the water is scummy, discolored, or near mines, farms or floodwater **then** find another source; boiling does not remove chemicals or algal toxins.
- **If** a hazard hits (common here: weather-flood, weather-extreme-heat, weather-winter-storm, weather-hurricane) **then** open that weather card.

## Don't
- Don't hunt, fish or trap without the right license, season and method. Survival needs do not override the law.
- Don't eat wild mushrooms without in-person expert confirmation.
- Don't use grills, generators or fuel heaters indoors (carbon monoxide).

## Get help now
Emergency: **911**. Poison Help: **1-800-222-1222**. Food, shelter and utility help: **211**. Rules and licenses: https://doee.dc.gov/ (Hunting is effectively not an option in DC; fishing needs a DC license and fish-eating advisories apply.)
