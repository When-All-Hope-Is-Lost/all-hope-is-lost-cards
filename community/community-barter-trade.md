## Do this now
**Trade in public with a partner, and never show your full supply.**

## Steps
1. Skills trade well: repair, sewing, medical help, cooking, childcare, gardening.
2. Goods that tend to keep value: water treatment, salt, seeds, soap, batteries, basic tools, canned food (opinion).
3. Agree on the full deal before handing anything over.
4. Meet in daylight, in a public or neutral place, with someone you trust.
5. Bring only what you plan to trade.
6. Inspect food and medicine for seals and dates.

## If / then
- **If** a deal feels pushy or rushed **then** walk away
- **If** someone wants to see your home or supplies **then** decline

## Don't
- Don't trade away your last water, medicine or warm clothing.
- Don't trade prescription drugs; it is illegal and dangerous.

## Get help now
- Threats or robbery: 911 when possible.
