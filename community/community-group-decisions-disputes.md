## Do this now
**Agree on simple written rules and who decides what, before conflict starts.**

## Steps
1. Pick roles: coordinator, food and water, medical, security/watch, supplies record keeper.
2. Write down a few rules everyone agrees to: sharing, work, safety, how decisions are made.
3. Make decisions in a short regular meeting. Let everyone speak before deciding.
4. Keep a public list of supplies and who used what.
5. For a dispute: separate the people, let each speak without interruption, restate what each wants, look for a fair trade or a rotation, write down the agreement.
6. Use a neutral third person to mediate when the two can't agree.
7. Rotate unpleasant jobs so no one carries them all.

## If / then
- **If** a dispute turns to threats or weapons **then** separate people, step back to safety, de-escalate calmly, call 911 if possible
- **If** someone repeatedly breaks safety rules **then** the group decides consequences together, in the open

## Don't
- Don't let one person control all food or medicine with no records.
- Don't punish people in secret or with violence.

## Get help now
- Violence or threats: 911.
