## Do this now
**Work outdoors or with strong ventilation, with eye, face and hand protection.**

## Steps
1. Learn from an experienced smith or class before starting.
2. Set up the forge on a non-flammable surface with clear space and a fire extinguisher or water and sand.
3. Wear safety glasses; for arc welding, a welding helmet with the correct shade lens. Bystanders need protection too.
4. Wear natural-fiber clothing, leather gloves and boots.
5. Treat all metal as hot. Mark hot pieces.
6. Ventilate: welding fumes and coal smoke are toxic.

## If / then
- **If** you get flu-like chills after heating galvanized metal **then** that's metal fume fever; stop, get fresh air, seek care; never heat galvanized or zinc-coated metal
- **If** a burn is larger than your palm or on face/hands **then** get medical care

## Don't
- Don't weld or forge in an enclosed space.
- Don't heat galvanized, painted or plated metal.
- Don't look at a welding arc without the right lens.

## Get help now
- Serious burns, eye injuries or breathing trouble: 911.
