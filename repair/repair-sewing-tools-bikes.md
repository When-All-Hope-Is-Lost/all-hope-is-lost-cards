## Do this now
**Fix small damage early; a patched hole or a sharp tool saves work and injuries.**

## Steps
1. Sewing: patch holes from the inside, use a backstitch for strong seams, reinforce knees and seat. Keep needles, strong thread and safety pins.
2. Sharpening: clamp the tool, hold a steady angle, push the edge into the stone, same strokes on both sides. Sharp tools slip less.
3. Axes and saws: keep handles tight, edges covered when stored, oil against rust.
4. Bike flat: remove the wheel, lever off the tire, find the hole (pump and listen or use water), patch or replace the tube, check the tire for the thorn, reinflate to the pressure on the tire sidewall.
5. Bike chain: keep it clean and oiled; a chain tool and spare links help.
6. Keep a basic kit: screwdrivers, pliers, wrenches, duct tape, wire, glue.

## If / then
- **If** a tool handle is cracked **then** replace it before use
- **If** brakes feel soft on a bike **then** fix them before riding

## Don't
- Don't sharpen toward your hand.
- Don't ride with a damaged rim or loose wheel.

## Get help now
- Deep cut or bleeding that won't stop: see the severe bleeding card and call 911.
