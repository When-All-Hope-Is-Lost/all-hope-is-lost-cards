## Do this now
**Bear: don't run.** Stand your ground, talk calmly, and back away slowly. **Moose: it's OK to run.** Get behind a tree, car, or building.

## Steps
**Bears**
1. **Bear hasn't noticed you?** Quietly move away and give it plenty of room.
2. **Bear notices you:** stay calm and **don't run**. Bears chase fleeing animals and can reach 35 mph. Talk in a calm voice so it knows you're a human, and back away slowly while watching it. Don't climb a tree.
3. **Curious bear keeps coming closer?** Group together, look big, and be loud and aggressive. Get bear spray ready and use it if the bear comes within range.
4. **Charge:** stand your ground. Most charges are bluffs that stop short. Don't run.

**Moose**
5. **Give moose lots of room** (at least 25 yards), especially a mother with a calf. Never get between them.
6. **Ears laid back and hackles up** means it's upset and may charge. Talk softly and move away.
7. **If it charges, run** and get behind something solid. If you're in the open, zigzag. Moose usually don't chase far.

## If / then
- **If** a **black bear** attacks **then** **fight back with everything you have.** Aim punches, kicks, rocks, or sticks at its face and nose. Don't play dead.
- **If** a **grizzly or brown bear** attacks **then** **play dead:** lie flat on your stomach with your legs apart, hands over the back of your neck, and pack on. Stay still and quiet until it has been gone for several minutes. **If the attack keeps going, fight back hard.**
- **If** a **moose** knocks you down **then** **curl into a ball, protect your head with your arms, and stay still.** Don't fight back. Move only after it has backed far away.
- **If** you're hurt **then** control bleeding (see the bleeding card) and get help.

## Don't
- Don't run from a bear, and don't climb a tree to escape one.
- Don't approach or feed bears or moose, or get close for a photo.
- Don't fight a moose that has knocked you down.

## Get help now
**911** for any injury from a bear or moose. Report aggressive wildlife to the state wildlife agency.
