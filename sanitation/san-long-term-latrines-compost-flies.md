## Do this now
**Put toilets well away and downhill from any water source, and wash hands after every use.**

## Steps
1. Choose a spot downhill and far from wells, streams and kitchens (about 100 feet is commonly advised).
2. Dig a pit latrine: narrow, deep trench or pit; cover waste with soil or ash after each use.
3. Add a cover or lid and a handwash station with soap.
4. When within about a foot of the top, fill with soil and dig a new pit.
5. Kitchen and garden scraps can be composted. Keep human and pet waste out of food-garden compost.
6. Flies spread disease: cover food, cover waste, bury trash, keep latrines covered.

## If / then
- **If** diarrhea spreads in the group **then** check water treatment, handwashing and latrine siting at once
- **If** the ground is rocky or water is high **then** use a bucket toilet with sawdust or soil and bury contents away from water

## Don't
- Don't put latrines near water or uphill from camp.
- Don't use human waste on food crops.

## Get help now
- Bloody diarrhea, high fever or signs of dehydration: get medical help.
