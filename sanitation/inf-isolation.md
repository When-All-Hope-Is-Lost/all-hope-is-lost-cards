## Do this now
Stay home and away from others if you have symptoms like fever, cough, or runny nose that are not better explained by another cause.

## Steps
1. **Stay home and away from others,** including people you live with who aren't sick. Common symptoms: fever, chills, cough, sore throat, runny or stuffy nose, aches, tiredness, headache.
2. **Return to normal activities** when, for at least 24 hours, both are true: your symptoms are getting better overall, and you've had no fever without using fever-reducing medicine.
3. **Take added precautions** for the next 5 days: cleaner air, good hygiene, a well-fitted mask, keeping your distance, and testing if you can when you're around others. You can still spread the virus after you feel better.
4. **Protect people at higher risk** of severe illness by taking extra steps like cleaner air and better hygiene.
5. **Stay home again** if your fever returns or you feel worse after returning.
6. **If you tested positive but never had symptoms**, take added precautions for 5 days.
7. **At home while sick**, consider cleaner air, good hygiene, and distancing to protect others, especially if you can't stay apart.
8. **Know these rules are for common respiratory viruses** (like flu, COVID and colds). Other diseases may have different rules, so follow a doctor or health department if they tell you otherwise.
9. **Monitor symptoms** and seek emergency care if you have trouble breathing, chest pain, or other warning signs.

## If / then
- **If** you have symptoms of a respiratory virus **then** stay home and away from others, including people you live with who are not sick.
- **If** for at least 24 hours your symptoms are improving and you've had no fever without fever medicine **then** you may return to normal activities, but take added precautions for the next 5 days.
- **If** you feel worse or your fever returns **then** stay home again and follow the same rules.
- **If** you tested positive but never had symptoms **then** take added precautions for 5 days.

## Don't
- Don't go to work, school, or public places if you're sick.
- Don't ignore symptoms that get worse or return.
- Don't skip precautions even after feeling better.

## Get help now
- **911**: if you have emergency symptoms like trouble breathing or chest pain.
- **Caring for someone sick:** see the home care card for warning signs that need care right away.
- **Local health department**: for guidance on testing and isolation rules.
