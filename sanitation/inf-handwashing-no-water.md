## Do this now
Soap and water is best. If you don't have it, use hand sanitizer with at least 60% alcohol. If you only have water, rub your hands together under the cleanest water you have, then dry them on a clean towel or let them air dry.

## Steps
1. **Check the label** on the hand sanitizer to make sure it has at least 60% alcohol.
2. **Apply enough** to cover all surfaces of both hands.
3. **Rub your hands together** until they feel dry, about 20 seconds.
4. **Do not rinse or wipe** your hands before they are dry.
5. **Supervise young children** when using hand sanitizer.
6. **Know when it matters most:** before eating or making food, after using the bathroom or changing diapers, after coughing, sneezing or blowing your nose, and before and after caring for someone who is sick.
7. **Know its limits:** sanitizer doesn't kill every germ. Soap and water work better against norovirus (stomach bug), Cryptosporidium, C. diff, and chemicals like pesticides or lead.

## If / then
- **If** your hands are visibly dirty or greasy **then** sanitizer may not work well. Wash with soap and water, or at least rub them under the cleanest water you have.
- **If** you are using hand sanitizer with children **then** make sure they do not put it in their mouths or eyes. Store it out of reach of children and pets.
- **If** you are in a place with little water **then** use the cleanest water available. Wash with soap and water for at least 20 seconds when possible.

## Don't
- Don't use hand sanitizer on visibly dirty or greasy hands.
- Don't let children drink hand sanitizer or put it in their eyes.
- Don't use hand sanitizer that has food flavors or smells like food, as it may be dangerous if swallowed.
- Don't make homemade hand sanitizer. CDC doesn't recommend it, and FDA warns it can be useless or cause skin burns.
- Don't use hand sanitizer near heat, flames, or sparks until your hands are completely dry.

## Get help now
- **Poison Help Line**: call 1-800-222-1222 if someone swallows hand sanitizer or has a serious reaction to it.
- **FDA’s List of Hand Sanitizers Consumers Should Not Use**: check at [www.fda.gov/handsanitizerlist](https://www.fda.gov/handsanitizerlist) before buying or using hand sanitizer.
