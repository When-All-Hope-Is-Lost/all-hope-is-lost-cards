## Do this now
**If you're lost, stop, stay calm, and figure out where you are before moving.**

## Steps
1. Get paper topographic maps of your area (USGS) and a baseplate compass; they need no power.
2. Orient the map: turn it until map north lines up with compass north, adjusted for declination printed on the map.
3. Take a bearing: point the compass's direction arrow at the target, turn the dial until the needle sits in the orienting arrow, read the number.
4. Follow a bearing by picking landmarks along the line and walking to each.
5. Use terrain: streams, ridges and roads are 'handrails' to follow.
6. Keep track of time and pace to estimate distance.

## If / then
- **If** you are lost and someone knows your plan **then** stay put, make yourself visible, signal
- **If** no compass **then** the sun rises roughly east and sets roughly west

## Don't
- Don't rely only on a phone GPS; batteries die.
- Don't hold the compass near metal or electronics.

## Get help now
- Lost and in danger: call 911; text 911 where available.
