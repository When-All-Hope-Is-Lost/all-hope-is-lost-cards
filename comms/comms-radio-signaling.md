## Do this now
**Get a battery or hand-crank NOAA weather radio first; it receives alerts with no license.**

## Steps
1. FRS walkie-talkies: no license, short range.
2. GMRS: more power and repeaters; needs an FCC license (no test).
3. CB: no license, channel 9 is for emergencies and traveler help.
4. Ham (amateur) radio: longest reach and emergency nets; needs an FCC license exam. Local radio clubs teach and test.
5. Agree on a channel, call times and a check-in plan with family.
6. Signals: three of anything (three fires, three whistle blasts, three flashes) means distress. A mirror flash can be seen far away. Large ground markings help aircraft.

## If / then
- **If** you have a license and an emergency **then** you may use any means to call for help when life is in danger (FCC)
- **If** power is out **then** use rechargeable batteries with a small solar charger

## Don't
- Don't transmit on ham or GMRS without a license except in a real emergency.
- Don't waste batteries; turn radios off between check-ins.

## Get help now
- 911 by phone or text whenever possible.
