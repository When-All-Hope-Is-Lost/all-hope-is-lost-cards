## Do this now
**Under a Winter Storm Warning, get to shelter and stay off the roads.** Heat safely: **no grills or generators indoors, and never heat with a gas stove or oven.**

## Steps
1. **Know the alerts:**
   - **Winter Storm Warning:** heavy snow, freezing rain, or sleet is coming soon or already happening. Warnings are usually issued 12 to 24 hours ahead.
   - **Watch:** a winter storm is possible, usually 12 to 48 hours out.
   - **Advisory:** conditions will be a significant inconvenience, and could become life-threatening without caution.
2. **Stay home if you can.** If you must go out, wear layers, limit your time outside, and watch for frostbite and hypothermia (see the cold-injury card).
3. **Heat safely.** Generators and grills go **outdoors only**, at least 20 feet from windows, doors, and attached garages. **Never heat your home with a gas stovetop or oven.**
4. **Use working smoke and carbon monoxide alarms** with battery backup.
5. **Shovel slowly and take breaks.** Overexertion in snow can trigger a heart attack.
6. **Gather supplies** for several days without power: medicine, water, food, flashlights, batteries, and whatever your pets need.

## If / then
- **If** you're stuck in your car **then** **stay in the vehicle** (see the vehicle card): run the engine about 10 minutes an hour, keep the exhaust pipe clear, crack a window, and make yourself visible.
- **If** your car starts to skid **then** ease off the gas and steer where you want the front of the car to go. With anti-lock brakes, press the brake steadily and don't pump it.
- **If** you can't afford heat **then** call 2-1-1 and ask about LIHEAP (see the utility card).

## Don't
- Don't use a gas oven, stovetop, grill, or camp stove to heat your home.
- Don't drive unless you have to. Weather-related crashes kill thousands of people a year.
- Don't touch a wet generator.

## Get help now
**911** for carbon monoxide symptoms, hypothermia, or chest pain while shoveling. **2-1-1** for warming centers.

**Outside the US** (official sites, checked 2026-10-16):
- **Emergency number:** UK **999 or 112** · Ireland **112 or 999** · Canada **911** (Nunavut and a few other places use local numbers) · Australia **000** (112 also works from a mobile; you can't text 000) · New Zealand **111**.
- **Official warnings:** UK **Met Office** (yellow, amber, red) · Ireland **Met Éireann** (met.ie) · Canada **Environment and Climate Change Canada** (weather.gc.ca or the WeatherCAN app; yellow, orange, or red Warnings, Advisories, and Watches) · Australia **Bureau of Meteorology** (bom.gov.au), plus state emergency services (Advice, Watch and Act, Emergency Warning) · New Zealand **MetService** (metservice.com; Watches, then Orange and Red Warnings), plus local Civil Defence.
- **Terms:** The Met Office warns separately for snow and ice, and MetService warns for heavy snow. Canada has separate alerts for winter storms, snow squalls, freezing rain, and blizzards.
