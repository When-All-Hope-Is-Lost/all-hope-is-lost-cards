## Do this now
**Follow your state and local water-use restrictions,** and cut water use wherever you can.

## Steps
1. **Check restrictions** with your state or local government or water provider.
2. **Bathroom:** take short showers instead of baths, turn the tap off while brushing or shaving, and don't flush unnecessarily (put tissues in the trash).
3. **Kitchen and laundry:** run the dishwasher and washer only when full, wash vegetables in a pan instead of under the tap, and **catch the water** you run while waiting for it to get hot to use on plants.
4. **Reuse water:** never pour usable water down the drain when it could go on plants or the garden.
5. **Fix leaks.** A faucet dripping once per second wastes about **2,700 gallons a year**.

## If / then
- **If** you must water a lawn or garden **then** do it early in the morning or in the evening, in several short sessions. Check the soil first: if it's still moist, skip it.
- **If** the drought is extreme **then** let the lawn go and save water for **trees and large shrubs**.
- **If** you have a well pump that cycles on when no water is being used **then** you have a leak. Get it fixed.

## Don't
- Don't leave hoses or sprinklers running unattended. A hose can pour out **600 gallons or more** in a few hours.
- Don't hose down driveways or sidewalks. Use a broom.
- Don't thaw food under running water.

## Get help now
Ask your local water provider about restrictions and help. Drought watch: **US Drought Monitor** (droughtmonitor.unl.edu) and **drought.gov**. If you run out of safe water, see the water cards.

**Outside the US** (official sites, checked 2026-10-16):
- **Emergency number:** UK **999 or 112** · Ireland **112 or 999** · Canada **911** (Nunavut and a few other places use local numbers) · Australia **000** (112 also works from a mobile; you can't text 000) · New Zealand **111**.
- **Official warnings:** UK **Met Office** (yellow, amber, red) · Ireland **Met Éireann** (met.ie) · Canada **Environment and Climate Change Canada** (weather.gc.ca or the WeatherCAN app; yellow, orange, or red Warnings, Advisories, and Watches) · Australia **Bureau of Meteorology** (bom.gov.au), plus state emergency services (Advice, Watch and Act, Emergency Warning) · New Zealand **MetService** (metservice.com; Watches, then Orange and Red Warnings), plus local Civil Defence.
- **Terms:** UK and Ireland water companies may announce a 'hosepipe ban' (temporary use ban). Australia uses staged water restrictions. Check with your local water provider.
