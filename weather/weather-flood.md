## Do this now
**Get to higher ground now.** Never walk, swim, or drive into flood water. **Turn Around, Don't Drown!**

## Steps
1. **Evacuate immediately if told to.** Never drive around barricades.
2. **Move to higher ground, or to a higher floor** if you're trapped in a building.
3. **Stay out of flood water.** **Six inches of moving water can knock you down**, and **one foot of moving water can sweep a car away**.
4. **Stay off bridges** over fast-moving water. They can wash out without warning.
5. **Listen** to the Emergency Alert System, NOAA Weather Radio, or local alerts.

## If / then
- **If** your car is trapped in fast-moving water **then** stay inside. If water rises inside the car, **get on the roof**.
- **If** you're trapped in a building **then** go to the highest level. Go on the roof only if you have to, and signal for help. **Don't climb into a closed attic**, where rising water can trap you.
- **If** you're returning after a flood **then** go back only when officials say it's safe. Don't touch wet electrical equipment, or anything electrical while standing in water. Snakes and other animals may be inside.
- **If** you use a generator afterward **then** run it only outdoors, away from windows.

## Don't
- **Don't drive through flooded roads or around barricades.** One foot of moving water can carry a car away.
- Don't wade in floodwater. It can carry sewage, chemicals, and debris, and downed power lines can electrify it.
- Don't let children help with flood cleanup. People with asthma or weak immune systems should stay out of moldy buildings.

## Get help now
**911** if you're trapped or someone is in the water. Treat drinking water if officials say it's unsafe (see the water card).

**Outside the US** (official sites, checked 2026-10-16):
- **Emergency number:** UK **999 or 112** · Ireland **112 or 999** · Canada **911** (Nunavut and a few other places use local numbers) · Australia **000** (112 also works from a mobile; you can't text 000) · New Zealand **111**.
- **Official warnings:** UK **Met Office** (yellow, amber, red) · Ireland **Met Éireann** (met.ie) · Canada **Environment and Climate Change Canada** (weather.gc.ca or the WeatherCAN app; yellow, orange, or red Warnings, Advisories, and Watches) · Australia **Bureau of Meteorology** (bom.gov.au), plus state emergency services (Advice, Watch and Act, Emergency Warning) · New Zealand **MetService** (metservice.com; Watches, then Orange and Red Warnings), plus local Civil Defence.
- **Terms:** Australia: for flood and storm help (not life-threatening), call the State Emergency Service (SES) on 132 500. UK: England, Scotland, Wales, and Northern Ireland each have their own flood-warning agency, alongside Met Office rain warnings.
